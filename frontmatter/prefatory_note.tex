\chapter*{Prefatory Note}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Prefatory Note}

The prefatory note is an optional section where you can provide important disclaimers, permissions, acknowledgments, or clarifications about your dissertation.

\section*{Typical Uses}

A prefatory note might include:
\begin{itemize}
\item Permission statements for previously published material
\item Important notes about data sources or methodological decisions
\item Clarifications about the scope or limitations of your work
\item Attribution of collaborative contributions
\item Special acknowledgments or dedications
\end{itemize}

\section*{Professional Tone}

Maintain a formal, professional tone in this section. This is distinct from the acknowledgments page and should be used for substantive, content-related notes rather than personal thanks.
