\chapter*{Pre-text}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Pre-text}

The pre-text section provides introductory material that sets the stage for your dissertation. This optional section can be used to present preliminary information, context, or foundational concepts that are essential for understanding your work.

\section*{Purpose}

Use this section to:
\begin{itemize}
\item Introduce overarching themes or concepts
\item Provide historical context or background
\item Define key terminology or frameworks
\item Establish the intellectual foundation for your research
\end{itemize}

\section*{Content Guidelines}

Keep this section focused and concise. It should complement rather than duplicate information from your introduction chapter.
