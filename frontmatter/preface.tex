\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

The preface allows you to speak directly to your readers about your dissertation. It is an opportunity to explain your motivation, approach, and perspective on the work.

\section*{What to Include}

In your preface, consider discussing:
\begin{itemize}
\item Your personal motivation for pursuing this research
\item How this dissertation fits into your broader scholarly interests
\item The evolution of your thinking or approach
\item Key insights or turning points in your research process
\item Your hopes for the impact or application of your work
\end{itemize}

\section*{Tone and Style}

The preface is more personal than the introduction, allowing for some authorial voice while maintaining academic professionalism. It provides a bridge between the formal front matter and the academic content of your dissertation.

\section*{Length}

Keep the preface to 2-4 pages. It should be substantive but not lengthy.
